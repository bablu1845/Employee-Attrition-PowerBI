# Employee Attrition Analysis – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition, identify key workforce risk factors, and provide data-driven retention insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition and retention
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition
- Analyze the impact of overtime, income, tenure, work-life balance, and promotion opportunities
- Identify high-risk employees using a rule-based risk score
- Estimate potential attrition cost and potential savings
- Provide actionable employee retention recommendations

## 🗂️ Dataset

**IBM HR Employee Attrition Dataset**

- Employees: 1,470
- Employees who left: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Retention Rate: 83.88%
- Tool: Microsoft Power BI

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Employees working overtime show higher attrition risk.
- Compensation is an important area for retention analysis.
- Early-tenure employees require stronger onboarding and support.
- Promotion opportunities can influence employee retention.
- Rule-based risk scoring can help HR teams identify employees requiring attention.

## ⚠️ Limitations

The analysis is based on historical snapshot data. The risk score is a rule-based analytical framework and should not be interpreted as a validated predictive model.
